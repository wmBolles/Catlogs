About CatLogs

CatLogs gathers historical commands and system events from across your Linux machine and displays them in a searchable, sortable interface. It reads shell histories, auth and journal logs, boot/power events, session lock and login activity, live process data, scanner results, and PDF-ready audit exports.

All data stays on your machine. CatLogs makes no network connections and sends nothing anywhere. You can export filtered views to CSV or generate a professional PDF audit report for local security reviews.

Local-Only & Privacy-First: CatLogs runs entirely offline on your local system. No network requests are made, no telemetry is gathered, and no data ever leaves your device.
